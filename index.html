Before we begin

Common questions.

Every project is different, but these are the questions clients most often ask at the start.

Can you work with clients outside Nigeria?+

Yes. Our process supports remote discovery, reviews and delivery, making it practical to work with clients locally and internationally.

What if I am unsure which service I need?+

That is completely fine. Describe the problem and the outcome you want. We will help determine whether the best response is a website, analysis, model or a combination.

Do you provide solutions tailored to our organisation?+

Yes. Customer-centred delivery is central to our approach. Scope, features and technical choices are based on your users, processes, constraints and priorities.

How will cost and timeline be determined?+

After an initial discussion, we define the scope and provide a clear proposal. Cost and timing depend on complexity, content readiness, integrations, data condition and delivery requirements.
